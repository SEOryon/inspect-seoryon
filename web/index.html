Technical

Catch the issues that block indexing

See status codes, redirects, HTTPS, canonical tags, and meta-robots at a glance — the signals that decide whether a page can rank at all.

  • HTTP status & redirect chains
  • Canonical & meta-robots / X-Robots-Tag
  • HTTPS, viewport & charset
Meta

See exactly what Google and social see

Title, meta description, and Open Graph / Twitter cards — with live character counts and warnings when they're too long, too short, or missing.

  • Title & description length checks
  • Open Graph & Twitter card preview
  • Duplicate & missing-tag flags
Content

Check structure the way crawlers read it

A full H1–H6 outline, image alt-text coverage, and word count — so you can spot thin content and broken hierarchy before they cost you rankings.

  • H1–H6 heading outline
  • Image alt-text coverage
  • Word count & thin-content flag
Links

Map every link on the page

Internal versus external, nofollow versus followed, and a live broken-link check — with anchor text — so nothing leaks authority or 404s in silence.

  • Internal vs external split
  • Nofollow & anchor text
  • Broken-link detection
