Questions, answered

The free Inspect extension

Is Inspect really free — forever?

Yes. No trial timer, no credit card, no account. Inspect is built by SEOryon as a genuinely useful front door to our paid platform.

Does my data leave my browser?

No. Inspect reads the page you're already viewing and runs every check locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and any Chromium browser — Edge, Brave, Arc, and Opera. A Firefox build is on the roadmap.

How is Inspect different from SEOryon?

Inspect audits one page, on demand, for free. SEOryon works across your whole site continuously, tracks where you're cited in AI search, and writes the content that gets you found.

SEOryon vs. the alternatives

How is SEOryon different from a keyword tool or an AI writer?

Keyword tools hand you a list and stop; AI writers spit out generic text. SEOryon reads live search data, decides what's worth writing and why, then writes content built to rank — research and writing in one loop.

Do I still need Ahrefs or Semrush?

Most teams don't. SEOryon tracks the rankings, competitors, backlinks, and site vitals those tools show — then acts on them by publishing content. Keep them if you like; SEOryon is built to replace the busywork.

Why SEOryon instead of an agency or doing it manually?

An agency is slow and expensive; manual SEO eats your week. SEOryon runs the research-to-publish loop continuously at a fraction of the cost — and you approve everything.

AI search & GEO

What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?

GEO is optimizing to be cited in AI answers — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI — not just the ten blue links. As people ask AI instead of searching, being the source it quotes is the new front page.

How do I get cited in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI?

Publish clear, well-structured, genuinely useful content that answers real questions — exactly what LLMs quote. SEOryon writes for that, and tracks where you're already being cited.

How is ranking in AI answers different from ranking in Google?

Google ranks pages; AI answers cite sources inside a generated reply. The fundamentals overlap — quality, structure, authority — but AI rewards content that directly answers the question. SEOryon optimizes for both.

Trust & how it works

Is AI-written content penalized by Google?

No — Google rewards helpful content regardless of how it's made, and penalizes spam. SEOryon writes genuine, fact-grounded articles you review before publishing, not spun filler.

No. SEOryon shows you the data and writes the content; you publish and stay in full control of your own site. We never edit your site for you.

Who controls what gets published?

You do. On semi-autopilot you approve every piece with one click; on full autopilot SEOryon publishes on your rules. Either way, nothing goes live without your say.
