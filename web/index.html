Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes — Inspect is free forever. No trial timer, no credit card, no account. It's built by SEOryon as a genuinely useful front door to our paid platform.

Does my data leave my browser?

No. Inspect reads the page you're already viewing and runs every check locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and any Chromium browser — Edge, Brave, Arc, and Opera. A Firefox build is on the roadmap.

How is it different from SEOryon?

Inspect audits one page, on demand, for free. SEOryon monitors your whole site continuously, tracks where you're cited across AI search, and writes the content that gets you ranked.
